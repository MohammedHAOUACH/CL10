We present \textbf{Causal Clifford Theory (CL10)}, a unitary quantum cellular
automaton on a Planck lattice whose on-site internal space is the
32-dimensional module of the Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}(10,0)$. Ten
mutually anticommuting Hermitian generators supply the kinematics. From this
single algebraic object follow a $(1{+}3)$ Minkowski subalgebra; a
16-component Weyl module whose charge assignment is exactly one Standard
Model generation, with all eight gauge and mixed anomalies cancelling; a
45-dimensional $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ adjoint containing the twelve gauge
bosons; the $5/3$ hypercharge normalisation; an exactly unitary, strictly
causal update with $\det U = 1$ and an exact matter--antimatter spectral
mirror; and a lattice theory of a massless spin-2 field whose discrete
Einstein--Hilbert action reproduces general relativity to machine precision.

We also state \textbf{what does not follow}. Every number is classified by
provenance --- algebraic, conventional, fitted, or open. The family number,
the Yukawa structure, quark mixing, the cosmological constant, the
dark-matter identity and abundance, the exact neutrino mass, the baryon
asymmetry and one-loop coupling unification remain open. One loop factor
$(1/16\pi)^{10}$ reproduces the electroweak/Planck hierarchy to a factor
$2.08$ and gives a $118$ GeV dark-matter scale, but the exponent $10$ is
selected by the data it is meant to explain.

Five tensions with observation are reported rather than hidden: the GUT
coupling $\alpha_{10} = 1/25$ run down with the Standard Model
$\beta$-functions produces a $U(1)$ Landau pole at $\sim 280$ GeV; the
seesaw value $m_\nu = 0.146$ eV exceeds the oscillation scale and the bound
$\sum m_\nu < 0.12$ eV; the relic abundance is normalised, not computed; the
heavy-neutrino decay is \emph{in} equilibrium ($\Gamma/H \simeq 4.6$); and
the computed Brillouin-zone spectrum is gapless at twelve doubler momenta.
The seven criteria, nine open residuals and seven falsifiers are stated
explicitly.
