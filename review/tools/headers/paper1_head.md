## Abstract

A quantum cellular automaton is defined on a Planck lattice whose $2{\times}2{\times}2$ cells carry the Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}(10,0)$ on eight sub-sites (on-site space $\mathbb{C}^{256}$) and are updated by one six-stage product of $\mathrm{Spin}(10)$ plane rotations and swap-rotations. From this root follow, with no added field: a $(1{+}3)$ Minkowski subalgebra; the $16$-component Weyl module whose charges are exactly one Standard Model generation, all eight gauge and mixed anomalies cancelling; the $\mathbf{45}$ adjoint containing the twelve gauge bosons; the $5/3$ hypercharge normalisation. The step is exactly unitary ($\det U = 1$, $\pm$-paired spectrum, exact matter/antimatter spectral mirror) and strictly causal, with measured front speed exactly one Planck cell per stage. The doubling of the lattice spectrum is displayed, not hidden: twelve gapless nodes at the half-zone momenta, gapped by a Wilson factor of the root's own algebra, with the split-node set an input (O9). The closure axiom — the update must be an element of the root — quantizes the rotation angle to $g \in \{\pi/2,\pi/4\}$: the fundamental step has order $8$, the internal spectrum is the finite algebraic set $\{\pm\pi/4\,(10),\ \pm3\pi/4\,(6)\}$, and the on-site update has zero continuous unfixed numbers. The tensions are named, not hidden: the doubler cones are linear in every sampled direction but anisotropic (velocity-tensor anisotropy $0.748$–$0.758$; $0$ of $2726$ gapless points of the Wilson family reach $A < 10^{-6}$; three nodal planes, not a cone, at the exactly gapless corner multiplet; frame averaging over the ten generators is blind to the anisotropy; and no bond of the $880$-point entangling programme is both gapless and non-commuting — a rigidity wall at gap $8\times10^{-3}$ over the axis-covariant class, which Proposition 6 explains as a parity no-go and escapes outside the class, on a parity-degenerate node that the engine's own six-stage product is searched for and does not reach, Section 8.4), and at the closed angle the gapless set becomes six nodal lines and the per-node chirality bookkeeping must be redone. The gauged bond layer, the gravitational sector and the phenomenology of this root are constructed and audited in companion papers [Paper II, Paper III].

### Summary of results

The determinism claim is interpretive: one time-independent local rule fixes every future state of the lattice — 't Hooft's cellular-automaton determinism exact at the fundamental update — with randomness entering only through the measurement projection; the projection and the Born rule are inputs of the interpretation (O9), not derived from the unitary step. The spin–statistics identification (even sector bosonic, odd sector fermionic) is likewise a postulate of the interpretation, not a derived theorem.

The results of this paper are the kinematics (Theorem 1), the matter content and anomaly freedom (Proposition 1, Theorem 2), the gauge content and the $5/3$ (Theorem 3), the exact unitarity and the causal radius (Section 7, Theorem 5), the spectral mirror (Theorem 4), the doubling and its Wilson gapping (Proposition 2, Section 8.1), the Lorentz structure at the nodes with its measured anisotropy (Section 8.2), the massless-mode census (Section 8.3), the cone no-go and the parity theorem (Propositions 4–6, Section 8.4), and the quantized clock with its measured costs (Theorem 6, Section 9). The loop-factor ladder, the coupling unification, the dark-matter and neutrino audits, and the full provenance audit are carried by Paper III; the gauged bond layer and the lattice gravitational sector by Paper II.
